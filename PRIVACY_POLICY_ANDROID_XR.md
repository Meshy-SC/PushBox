- **App:** Push Box (Push Box: Sokoban Puzzle on Google Play)
- **Publisher:** Meshy (Ahmed Dalhi)
- **Last updated:** November 13, 2026

## Summary

Push Box has no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. Your progress and settings stay on your device. We cannot see them.

Push Box is free to download and sells add-ons through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; the app only learns which add-ons you own, on your device, so that it can unlock them.

To put the puzzle on your floor, the app can use your headset's scan of the room and the position of your head. Both are optional, both are used on your device only, and the game plays without them.

Any user can ask us to delete their data at any time. Section 7 explains how.

## 2. Data the App Uses and How

### 2.1 Your Push Box purchases (Google Play)

The first ten levels of the Iron world and the first level of every other world are free. In the app you can buy:

- each world (Iron, Bronze, Silver and Gold), which unlocks all of its levels for good
- packs of solution credits; one credit unlocks one level's solution for good

What happens:

- **Payment** happens entirely in Google Play's own purchase screen, with your Google account. We never see or receive your payment details, your name, your email address or your billing address.
- **What the app receives from Google Play, on your device:** the prices of these add-ons in your currency, the list of add-ons your Google account bought in Push Box, and a purchase reference for each one.
- **How it is used:** to open the worlds you own, to add the credits of each pack exactly once, and to restore your purchases after a reinstall or on another device (**Settings → Restore Purchases**). The app does not send this information anywhere.

The app talks to Google Play through the Google Play Billing library, which is Google's code inside the app. Google handles purchases, and anything that library reports to Google, under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 2.2 Mixed reality: your room, your head and your hands

- **Passthrough.** The app shows the puzzle on the floor of your room over the headset's view of the room. Android XR draws that view itself: the app never receives, saves or sends camera images.
- **Room scan (optional).** If you allow it, the app asks Android XR for the flat surfaces it has found in your room and uses the height of the floor to stand the puzzle on it. Only that height is used, on the spot. It is never saved or sent. If you refuse, the app estimates the floor and you can drag the puzzle with the bar under the controls.
- **Head position (optional).** If you allow it, the app reads where your head is while you play, to put the puzzle where you look and to turn the controls toward the side of the puzzle you stand on. It is used on the spot and is never saved or sent. If you refuse, the controls stay on the side where you placed the puzzle.
- **Hands and pointing.** When you pinch a button, an arrow or the bar that moves the puzzle, Android XR tells the app what you selected and, while you drag the puzzle, the direction you point in. This is used on the spot and is never saved or sent. The app does not read the shape of your hands or track your eyes.

Before your headset asks for the room scan and the head position, the app explains what they are for. You can change your answer at any time in the headset's settings.

### 2.3 Emails you send us

If you email us for support or to request deletion, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you include. We use it only to answer you or to handle your request. Your email passes through our email provider. We keep it only while we handle your request. For deletion requests, we delete your email once we have confirmed the deletion to you.

## 3. Data That Stays on Your Device

To keep your game going, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device. **It is not sent to us, and we cannot see it.**

- Level completions, best push counts and star ratings
- Your sound settings and the board size you chose
- Your solution credits, the levels whose solution you unlocked, and the references of the credit packs already added, so that a pack is never added twice
- The add-ons your Google account owns: a copy of Google Play's answer, so the game also works offline
- Whether the app has already explained the room scan and head position to you

**Android backup.** If backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in the backup Google keeps for you, so that it can be restored on another device. Google runs that backup under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. Data We Do Not Collect

We collect nothing. In particular, Push Box does **not** collect:

- Your name, email address or phone number (unless you email us, as in section 2.3)
- Your payment details
- Hardware serial numbers or advertising identifiers
- Location, whether precise or approximate
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Camera images, room data, hand or eye data, recordings or voice

The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting code of ours. If the app crashes and you have agreed to share diagnostics with Google, Android may report the crash to Google, and Google Play Console then shows us technical details: the device model, the Android and app versions, and where in the code the crash happened.

## 7. Requesting Data Deletion

**All users, in every country, can ask us to delete their data at any time. It is free, and you do not have to give a reason.**

### Data on your device

You can delete it yourself, immediately:

- In Push Box, **Settings → Reset All Progress** deletes your level completions, push counts and star ratings. Your purchases and solution credits are kept.
- **Uninstalling Push Box**, or clearing its storage in the device's settings, permanently deletes all of the app's data from your device. World purchases stay on your Google account: if you install the app again, **Settings → Restore Purchases** brings them back.

### Data held by Google

Your purchases are part of your Google account, and Google keeps them so that you can restore them. You can manage or delete the data Google holds about your account with Google's own tools, described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We hold no copy of this data.

### Ask us

Email **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** with the subject **"Data deletion request"**. Within **30 days**, we delete any emails you have sent us, help you remove the data described above, and confirm the deletion to you by email.

We will not refuse a deletion request. No law requires us to keep any of this data.

## 9. Network Activity

The game itself plays offline and opens no network connection of its own. The app uses the network only through Google Play and the Google Play Billing library, for purchases: prices, Google Play's purchase screen, the list of your purchases, and marking credit packs as delivered (section 2.1).

## 10. System Permissions

- **Room scan (`SCENE_UNDERSTANDING_COARSE`), optional:** the height of your floor (section 2.2).
- **Head position (`HEAD_TRACKING`), optional:** where you look and which side of the puzzle you stand on (section 2.2).
- **Google Play purchases (`com.android.vending.BILLING`) and Internet (`INTERNET`):** used only by the Google Play Billing library, to reach Google Play for purchases.
- **Network state and wake lock (`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `WAKE_LOCK`):** declared by Android libraries the app is built with. The game does not use them.

Push Box does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, eye tracking, hand tracking, files outside its own storage, or any advertising identifier.
