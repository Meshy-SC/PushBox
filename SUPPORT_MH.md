# Push Box Support (Meta Quest)

**Welcome to Push Box Support.** We are here to help you get the most out of your puzzle experience on Meta Quest.

## Supported Devices

- **Meta Quest 2**
- **Meta Quest Pro**
- **Meta Quest 3**
- **Meta Quest 3S**

Push Box is a mixed-reality game: the puzzle stands on the floor of your own room, through the headset's passthrough. Play standing or seated, with Touch controllers or with your hands.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**How do I play?**
Push every box onto a glowing goal tile. You can only push boxes, never pull them, and only one at a time, so plan ahead: a box pushed into a corner stays there. A box turns red when it sits on a goal.

**Where does the puzzle appear?**
The first time you open a level, a pulsing marker follows your gaze across the floor. Look where you want the puzzle and select **Tap to place your level**. Every level you play afterwards appears in the same spot, until you close the App.

**How do I move?**
Use the arrows floating above the puzzle, or either thumbstick. The arrows always work from where you stand: walk around the puzzle and "up" still means away from you. Each move steps the robot one tile.

**How do I choose a level?**
Push Box opens on the level browser. Pick a world, then a level. To come back to the browser, select the level button on the toolbar, or press **B** or **Y** on a controller.

**What do the toolbar buttons do?**
From left to right: level selection, board size (0.5×, 1×, 2× and 3×), restart the level, solution (plays the solution step by step; select it again to stop), undo the last move, previous level and next level.

**What is the gauge behind the puzzle?**
It counts your pushes against the two-star limit. It stays green while you are at or under par, turns orange up to 20% over par, and red beyond.

### Worlds, Stars & Scoring

**How many levels are there?**
400 puzzles in four worlds: Iron, Bronze, Silver and Gold, with 100 levels each. More worlds — Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Legend — are coming in future updates.

**How do I unlock levels?**
Every world is open from the start. Inside a world, levels come in groups of ten: complete six levels of a group to unlock the next one.

**How do I earn 3 stars?**
Finish a level in par, the fewest pushes possible. Within 20% above par earns two stars, and any other finish one star. Stars are worth 10, 7 and 5 points toward your score. Replaying a level never lowers a rating you already earned.

**Does the solution stop before the end?**
Yes. It stops one move short so that you make the winning push yourself.

### Settings

**Where are the settings?**
In the level browser, open **Settings**. You can turn the background music and each sound effect on or off, open this page and the privacy policy, reset your progress, and see the App's version.

### Data and Privacy

**What data does Push Box collect?**
None. Your progress and settings are stored on the headset only. For full details, see our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY_MH.md).

**How do I start over?**
Open **Settings → Reset All Progress**. This permanently deletes all level completions, star ratings and scores.

### Troubleshooting

**The App closes as soon as a level opens, or the puzzle does not appear.**
The headset needs to track its position in the room. Make sure the room is well lit and the headset's cameras are not covered, then open the level again.

**The puzzle is somewhere I can't see it.**
Return to the level browser, close Push Box, and open it again: the next level asks you to place the puzzle again.

**My hands do not press the buttons.**
Make sure hand tracking is turned on in the headset's settings, and keep your hand in view of the headset while you pinch.

**There is no sound.**
Check the switches in **Settings** and the headset's volume.

**The App is crashing or not responding.**
1. Close the App and reopen it.
2. Make sure your headset is running the latest Horizon OS version.
3. Update Push Box to the latest version from the Meta Horizon Store.
4. If the issue persists, contact us (see below).
