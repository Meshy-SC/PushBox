# Privacy Policy for Push Box on Meta Quest

**Last Updated:** November 9, 2026

## Overview

Push Box ("the App") is a Sokoban box-pushing puzzle game for Meta Horizon OS, available on Meta Quest 2, Meta Quest Pro, Meta Quest 3 and Meta Quest 3S. It is developed and published by Meshy ("we", "our", "us"). This policy covers the Meta Quest version of the App.

## Data Collection

Push Box does **not** collect, store or transmit any personal data to us. We operate no servers, no databases and no back end of any kind, and we have no access to your information. Purchases are made through the Meta Horizon Store and handled by Meta (see **In-App Purchases** below).

### We Do NOT Collect
- Personal identification information
- Location data
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Advertising identifiers
- Crash reports
- Camera images, room data or recordings
- Any data whatsoever

## Data Stored on Your Headset

To keep your game going, the App saves the following **on your headset only**, in its private app storage:

- Level completions, best push counts, star ratings and scores
- Your music and sound-effect settings and the board size you chose
- Your solution credits, the levels whose solution you unlocked, and which worlds you own (a copy of what the Meta Horizon Store reports, so the App also works offline)

**Earlier version of Push Box.** If you played the earlier version of Push Box on this headset, the App reads the progress file that version left in its own storage on your headset, once, to carry your completed levels over. Nothing is sent anywhere.

None of this is sent to us. You can erase your progress at any time from **Settings → Reset All Progress**, and uninstalling the App removes all of it from the headset.

**Meta cloud backup.** If cloud backup is turned on in your headset's settings, Horizon OS may include this saved progress in the backup Meta keeps for your account, so that it can be restored on another headset. That backup is run by Meta, not by us, and Meta's privacy policy applies to it: [meta.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/)

## Mixed Reality and Controllers

- **Passthrough.** The App places the puzzle on the floor of your room using the headset's passthrough view. Horizon OS draws the passthrough image itself: the App never receives, stores or transmits camera images or information about your room.
- **Head position.** To place the board where you look and to turn the controls toward the side of the board you are standing on, the App reads the headset's position from Horizon OS while you play. It is used on the spot and is never stored or transmitted.
- **Controllers.** The App reads your Touch controllers' buttons, thumbsticks and pointing direction from Horizon OS so that you can move the player and press the buttons. This is used on the spot and is never stored or transmitted.

## Third-Party Services

### Meta Horizon Platform
The App uses the Meta Horizon Platform SDK to check that your copy of the App was obtained through the Meta Horizon Store (an entitlement check the store requires) and for in-app purchases. Both are handled by Meta's software and servers, and Meta's privacy policy applies to them: [meta.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/)

### In-App Purchases
Push Box is free to download. The first ten levels of the Iron world and the first level of every other world are free; the rest of each world, and solution credits, can be bought in the App through the Meta Horizon Store.

- Payment happens entirely in Meta's own checkout. We never see or receive your payment details, your name or your email address.
- To know what you own, the App asks the Meta Horizon Platform SDK for your purchases. Meta identifies your account with your Meta Horizon user ID for this; the App receives only the list of items you own (such as "pushbox.world.2") and keeps nothing else.
- Buying or restoring purchases needs an internet connection. The game itself plays offline.

The App contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.

## Data Sharing

We do **not**:
- Sell your data.
- Share your data with third parties.
- Use your data for advertising.
- Track your behaviour across apps or sessions.

## Permissions Requested

- **Internet** — used only by the Meta Horizon Platform SDK to reach the Meta Horizon Store for purchases and the entitlement check.

Push Box does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, files outside its own storage, or any advertising identifier.

## Children's Privacy

Push Box does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
