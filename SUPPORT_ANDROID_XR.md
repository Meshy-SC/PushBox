# Push Box Support (Android XR)

**Welcome to Push Box Support.** We are here to help you get the most out of your puzzle experience on Android XR.

## Supported Devices

Android XR headsets, from Google Play.

Push Box is a mixed-reality game: the puzzle stands on the floor of your own room, over the headset's view of the room. Play standing or seated, with your hands.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**How do I play?**
Push every box onto a glowing goal tile. You can only push boxes, never pull them, and only one at a time, so plan ahead: a box pushed into a corner stays there. A box lights up when it sits on a goal.

**How do I choose a level?**
Push Box opens on the level browser, a window like any other app's. Pick a world, then a level: the app then takes over your view of the room to show the puzzle. To come back to the browser, select the level button on the toolbar (the grid, at the left of the buttons).

**Why does the app ask about my room and my head?**
The first time you open a level, Push Box explains two optional permissions, and your headset then asks for them:
- the headset's scan of the room, to find the height of your floor;
- the position of your head, to put the puzzle where you look and to turn the controls toward you as you walk around it.

Both are used on your device only, and the game plays without them. You can change your answer in the headset's settings (Apps → Push Box → Permissions).

**Where does the puzzle appear?**
A marker follows your gaze across the floor. Look where you want the puzzle and pinch **Tap to place your level**. Every level you play afterwards appears in the same spot, until you close the app. If you did not allow the head position, the marker waits a little in front of where you stood when you opened the app.

**How do I move the puzzle afterwards?**
Pinch the small bar on the floor under the toolbar and drag: the puzzle follows across the floor and turns to face you.

**How do I walk the robot?**
Pinch one of the four arrows beside the puzzle, once per step. Directions always work from where you stand: walk around the puzzle and "up" still means away from you. With a keyboard, use the arrow keys or W A S D; Z undoes, R restarts, and Escape goes back to the levels.

**What do the toolbar buttons do?**
From left to right: level selection, board size (1×, 2× and 3×), restart the level, solution (plays the solution step by step; select it again to stop), undo the last move, previous level and next level.

**What is the gauge behind the puzzle?**
It counts your pushes against the two-star limit. It stays green while you are at or under par, turns orange up to 20% over par, and red beyond.

### Worlds, Stars & Scoring

**How many levels are there?**
400 puzzles in four worlds: Iron, Bronze, Silver and Gold, with 100 levels each. More worlds — Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Legend — are coming in future updates.

**How do I unlock levels?**
Inside a world, levels come in groups of ten: complete six levels of a group to unlock the next one.

**How do I earn 3 stars?**
Finish a level in par, the fewest pushes possible. Within 20% above par earns two stars, and any other finish one star. Stars are worth 10, 7 and 5 points toward your score. Replaying a level never lowers a rating you already earned.

**Does the solution stop before the end?**
Yes. It stops one move short so that you make the winning push yourself.

### Free Levels and Purchases

**What is free?**
Push Box is free to download. The first ten levels of the Iron world, and the first level of the Bronze, Silver and Gold worlds, are free to play.

**How do I unlock a whole world?**
Each world is a one-time purchase that unlocks all 100 of its levels. Buy it from the banner above the world's levels, or when the game offers it after a free level. Purchases go through Google Play.

**What are solution credits?**
The solution button plays a level's solution. One credit unlocks a level's solution for good: replay it as often as you like. You get one credit free; packs of 1, 5, 10 and 25 credits are in **Settings → Purchases**, or offered when you press the solution button with no credit left.

**My purchase is missing.**
Make sure the device is online and signed in to the Google account that made the purchase, then open **Settings → Restore Purchases**. A payment that Google Play still shows as pending is added as soon as it completes. If a purchase is still missing, contact us (see below).

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Google Play: see "Request a refund" in Google Play's help, or contact us and we will help.

### Settings

**Where are the settings?**
In the level browser, open **Settings**. You can turn the background music and each sound effect on or off, buy solution credits, restore purchases, open this page and the privacy policy, reset your progress, and see the app's version.

### Data and Privacy

**What data does Push Box collect?**
None. Your progress, settings and solution credits are stored on the device only. Purchases go through Google Play, and the app only learns, on your device, which add-ons you own. The room scan and the head position are optional and never leave the device. Push Box has no servers, ads or analytics. For full details, including how to delete your data, see our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY_ANDROID_XR.md).

**How do I start over?**
Open **Settings → Reset All Progress**. This permanently deletes all level completions, star ratings and scores. Your purchases and solution credits are kept.

### Troubleshooting

**The puzzle does not sit on my floor.**
Allow the room scan (headset settings → Apps → Push Box → Permissions), then close Push Box and open it again. Without the scan the app estimates where the floor is.

**The puzzle is somewhere I can't see it.**
Return to the level browser, close Push Box, and open it again: the next level asks you to place the puzzle again.

**The controls stay on one side of the puzzle.**
They follow you only if you allowed the head position (headset settings → Apps → Push Box → Permissions).

**There is no sound.**
Check the switches in **Settings** and the headset's volume.

**The app is crashing or not responding.**
1. Close the app and reopen it.
2. Make sure your headset's system software is up to date.
3. Update Push Box to the latest version from Google Play.
4. If the issue persists, contact us (see below).
