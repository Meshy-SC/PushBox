Privacy Policy for Push Box (Apple TV)
Last updated: August 11, 2026

Overview
Push Box ("the Game", "the App") is a 3D Sokoban puzzle game developed by Meshy SC for Apple TV (tvOS).

We are committed to protecting the privacy of our players. Push Box is designed with a strict privacy-first principle: we do not collect, track, store, or sell any of your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you play Push Box on Apple TV, in full compliance with Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines (including Guideline 5.1.1) and global privacy standards.

1. Information Handled by the Game
Push Box processes only the minimal, non-identifiable data necessary to deliver the gameplay experience on your Apple TV:

A. Game Progress & High Scores
Your puzzle progress is stored locally on your Apple TV using Apple's standard storage frameworks (SwiftData and UserDefaults), including:

Completed levels across worlds (e.g., Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold)
High scores, push counts, and star ratings per level
Remaining solution credits and unlocked solutions
iCloud Synchronization (CloudKit): If your Apple TV is signed in to an Apple Account with iCloud enabled, the Game automatically syncs your progress with your other Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit framework. This data is stored strictly in your own private iCloud database. We do not host external servers, and we cannot access, view, or modify your iCloud data.

B. Game Center Integration
Push Box integrates with Apple’s Game Center on tvOS for achievements and global leaderboards:

When you achieve high scores or solve levels, your scores may be submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards.
Game Center operations are handled entirely by Apple’s operating system according to Apple's Privacy Policy. We never store or transmit your Game Center ID or profile on third-party servers.
C. Audio & Game Preferences
Your in-game settings (such as sound effects and background music volume/toggles) are stored locally on your Apple TV. They are never transmitted over the internet.

D. In-App Purchases (StoreKit)
If you make optional one-time purchases (such as unlocking additional worlds or purchasing solution credit packs):

All transactions are processed directly by Apple via StoreKit.
Push Box receives only the anonymous transaction confirmation from Apple to unlock the content on your Apple TV.
We never receive, process, or store your credit card details, billing address, or payment information.
2. Information We DO NOT Collect
Push Box does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data. Specifically:

No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or mailing addresses.
No Location Tracking: The Game does not request or access GPS or location data.
No Third-Party Analytics or SDKs: There are no third-party tracking, analytics, or profiling frameworks embedded in the Game.
No Advertising: The Game contains zero third-party advertisements or marketing trackers.
No Cross-App Tracking: We do not track you across apps or websites. The Game's Apple Privacy Manifest explicitly declares NSPrivacyTracking = false.
No Account Creation: You do not need to register an account or provide any credentials to play.
3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Family Friendly)
Push Box is designed for players of all ages and is rated 4+ on the tvOS App Store.

We comply fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or applicable legal age).
The Game contains no external web browsing, social media plugins, or unmoderated chat.
In-app purchases are subject to Apple’s built-in parental controls, Screen Time settings, and Ask to Buy on Apple TV.
4. Data Storage & Security
On-Device Storage: All game assets and level logic run locally on your Apple TV. Levels are generated directly on the device, requiring no continuous internet connection to play.
Secure Cloud Sync: Any cloud-synced progress is encrypted in transit and at rest within Apple's secure iCloud infrastructure.
No External Servers: We do not operate external web servers or databases holding user records.
5. Player Control & Data Deletion
You have complete control over all data generated by Push Box:

Reset In-Game Progress: You can reset all level completions, scores, and star ratings at any time inside the Game by navigating to Settings → Support & Data → Reset All Progress.
Delete Local Data: Deleting the Push Box app from your Apple TV removes all local game files and saved states.
Delete iCloud Data: You can delete your synced progress from your Apple Account at any time via your Apple TV or iOS settings (Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Push Box).
6. Third-Party Services
Push Box uses only native Apple-provided system frameworks:

Framework / Service	Provider	Purpose
iCloud (CloudKit)	Apple Inc.	Private cross-device progress synchronization
Game Center	Apple Inc.	Achievements and leaderboards
StoreKit	Apple Inc.	Secure in-app purchases and purchase restoration
No non-Apple third-party services, ad networks, or data brokers are used.

7. Customer Support & Feedback
If you contact our support team by email, we receive only the message and details you voluntarily provide. We use your email address exclusively to respond to your support request and resolve technical issues. We will never sell, share, or use your email for marketing.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the Game's features or legal requirements change. Any updates will be published with a revised "Last updated" date at the top.
